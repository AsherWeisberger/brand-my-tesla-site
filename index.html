Questions

Straight answers.

What do I actually get?

A die-cut vinyl of your logo on the panel you won, sized to the spot. A linked listing on this page. And the car in Robert's daily miles and content. We don't promise impression counts. We promise the car is out there, and he films from it constantly.

Why this car?

Because it goes where the tech industry goes. Robert drives it to conferences, meetups, and Supercharger stops all over the Bay Area. The @Scobleizer feed is where a lot of those people already are. It's the most photographed car in his footage.

How does payment work?

A bid is a request. We email you a 20% deposit invoice to hold the panel. When the auction closes, winners pay the balance within seven days. Get outbid and the deposit is refunded right away. Same invoicing Unaligned already uses for sponsorships.

What if someone outbids me?

You get an email and can raise. Minimum raise is $50. Bids placed in the last ten minutes extend the clock by ten minutes so nobody gets sniped.

How long does the vinyl stay on?

Until the car is sold, or you ask it off. No three-month campaign. No “2026 season.” Two artwork swaps a year are included.

Can any brand join?

Almost. No politics, no adult content, no crypto pump schemes, nothing that would embarrass the driver. Robert has final say and will refund any bid he declines.

Will my logo look like the preview?

Very close. The preview maps your file onto the panel geometry from six angles and lights it with the paint. The vinyl shop cuts from the same file at the listed size.

Is the bid live for everyone?

Yes — every bid is emailed to us and held against the panel. The car on this page updates for you immediately. A shared live board is next; until then the invoice is the lock.
