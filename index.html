Questions

Straight answers.

Is this real?

Yes. The Model 3 is real, the vinyl is real, and Robert actually drives it. The only stretch is treating a car like premium ad inventory, which is the point.

Why this car?

Because it goes where the tech industry goes. Robert drives it to conferences, meetups, and Supercharger stops all over the Bay Area, and he films from it constantly. It's the most photographed car in his feed.

What do I actually get?

A die cut vinyl of your logo on the panel you won, sized to the spot. A linked listing on this page. And the car in Robert's daily miles and content. We don't promise impression counts, we promise the car is out there.

How does payment work?

A 20% deposit holds your bid. When the auction closes, winners pay the balance by invoice within seven days. Get outbid and your deposit is refunded right away.

What if someone outbids me?

You get an email and can raise. Minimum raise is $50. Bids placed in the last ten minutes extend the clock by ten minutes so nobody gets sniped.

Can any brand join?

Almost. No politics, no adult content, no crypto pump schemes, nothing that would embarrass the driver. Robert has final say and will refund any bid he declines.

Will my logo look like the preview?

Very close. The preview maps your file onto the real panel geometry from six angles and lights it with the paint. The vinyl shop cuts from the same file at the listed size.
